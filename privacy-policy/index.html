SORTVERSE — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · Developer: ir-As Games · Contact: irasyazilim@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the SORTVERSE mobile game ("the Game") collects and how it is used. It applies to the current version of the Game distributed through Google Play.

1. Information we collect

The current version of the Game does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The Game does not require an account or sign-in.
  • The Game does not contain advertising and does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • The Game does not include analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

2. Purchases

Future versions of the Game may offer optional in-app purchases. These are processed by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details. When purchases are introduced, we will receive only the information needed to deliver and verify the item you bought (for example, a purchase confirmation token). This policy will be updated before that happens.

3. Third parties

The Game is built with the Unity engine and distributed through Google Play. In its current version, the Game does not send personal information to us or to any third-party service. Google Play may collect information under its own policies when you download or update apps: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Future online features

We plan to add online features such as cloud save, friends, clubs, leaderboards, and multiplayer. Before any such feature is released, we will update this policy to describe exactly what data is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how you can request its deletion. We will also update the Google Play Data safety section at the same time.

6. Your rights

Because the current version collects no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in your device settings. For any privacy question, contact irasyazilim@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
