SORTVERSE — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Developer: ir-As Games · Contact: irasyazilim@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the SORTVERSE mobile game ("the Game") collects and how it is used. It applies to the Game for Android (version 1.1 and later), distributed through Google Play.

Changes in this version

  • New in section 4: optional cloud save and optional leaderboards through Google Play Games. Both work only if you sign in to Google Play Games, and the data goes to Google, not to us.
  • Sections 1, 5, 7 and 8 and the Turkish summary were updated to match.
  • Everything else (no ads, no analytics, nothing sent to us, purchases through Google Play, the time request) is unchanged.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information through the Game.

The only optional features that involve another party are the cloud save and the leaderboards in section 4. They store data in your own Google Play Games account at Google, not with us.

  • The Game does not require an account or sign-in. You can optionally sign in to Google Play Games to use the cloud save and the leaderboards (section 4).
  • The Game does not contain advertising and does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • The Game does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. Game events that the Game keeps for its own use (for example "level completed") stay in the device's memory while the Game runs. They are not saved to a file and not sent anywhere.
  • We have no server that receives data from the Game. (The optional cloud save and leaderboards go to Google, section 4.)

2. Purchases and subscriptions

The Game offers optional purchases: coin and gem packs, a Starter Pack, a Season Pass, limited-time offers and a monthly "VIP" subscription.

  • Google Play handles the payment. Purchases and subscriptions run through Google Play Billing under Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never see or receive your payment card or account details. Google tells us nothing about you beyond the confirmation the Game needs on your device.
  • What the Game keeps on your device. When Google Play confirms a purchase, the Game checks Google's digital signature on the confirmation, delivers the item once, and saves a small record in its local save file: the order identifier (or purchase token), the product name (for example gems_s) and whether the item was delivered. This record stops the same purchase from being delivered twice and lets the Game restore what you bought. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Restoring purchases. Items you bought that are not consumed (such as the Season Pass) and your VIP subscription are read again from Google Play when the Game starts and when you tap "Restore purchases". The answer comes from Google Play to your device.
  • Subscription. VIP renews automatically every month until you cancel it. You can cancel any time in Google Play (Menu, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions) or from the "Manage" button in the Game, which opens the Google Play subscription page. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies; if a purchase is refunded or a subscription ends, the Game removes the item or benefit the next time it reads your purchases.
  • Prices shown in the Game are the prices Google Play reports for your country and currency.

3. The time request

Limited-time offers and the Season Pass need a trustworthy date. When the Game starts, when you return to it, and about every six hours while it is open, it sends one HTTPS request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 and reads the date and time from the reply. Nothing is added to this request: no identifier, no cookie from the Game, no information about you, your device or your progress. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address and standard technical request information (such as the user-agent text of the Unity engine) and handles it under Google's privacy policy. We receive nothing. If the request fails (for example you are offline), the Game simply keeps playing and hides the time-limited features until a later request succeeds.

4. What stays on your device

Your progress, settings (language, sound, vibration and similar), collected rewards, decorations and the purchase record from section 2 are saved in a file in the Game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this file. If you use Android's device backup, Android or your device maker may copy app data to your own Google account under the device's backup settings; that is controlled by your device, not by us.

Cloud save (optional, Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Settings, Cloud save), SORTVERSE saves your game progress (levels, coins, gems, decorations and purchase records) to your Play Games account so you can restore it on a new phone. This data is stored and processed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email or account identifier. You can turn this off by not signing in. You can delete the saved data in Google Play Games settings (Play Games, Settings, Delete Play Games account & data) or by deleting the saved game there.

Leaderboards (optional, Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, SORTVERSE submits two scores to Google Play Games leaderboards: your total stars and your longest daily reward streak. Other players can see these scores together with your Play Games profile name and avatar, according to your own Play Games settings (profile visibility). The scores and the profile are stored and processed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email, account identifier or scores. You can leave the leaderboards by not signing in, and you can hide your profile or delete your Play Games data in Google Play Games settings.

5. Third parties

The Game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchasing package (for Google Play Billing). We do not use Unity's online services, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting features, and we do not send any data to Unity. The Game's only network contacts are Google Play (purchases, section 2), the Google time request (section 3) and, if you sign in, Google Play Games (cloud save and leaderboards, section 4), plus a link that opens the Google Play subscription page in your browser or the Play Store when you tap "Manage". Google Play may also collect information under its own policies when you download or update apps.

6. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by us for you to access, correct or delete. To remove everything the Game stored on your device, uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings (Settings, Apps, SORTVERSE, Storage, Clear data). Purchases are tied to your Google account and are managed with Google; after reinstalling you can restore them with "Restore purchases". Cloud save and leaderboard data are held by Google in your Play Games account; you can delete them in Google Play Games settings as described in section 4. For any privacy question, including questions under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK, contact irasyazilim@gmail.com.

8. Future online features

Cloud save and leaderboards are described in section 4. We plan to add further online features later, such as achievements, friends, clubs and multiplayer. Before any such feature is released, we will update this policy to describe exactly what data is collected, why, how long it is kept and how you can request its deletion. We will also update the Google Play Data safety section at the same time.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
